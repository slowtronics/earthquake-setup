EARTHQUAKE TRACKER

ESP32 + TFT · Live USGS Feed Setup Guide

 USGS Feed

Welcome to your ESP32 Earthquake Tracker! Once connected to Wi-Fi, it pulls live data straight from the USGS earthquake feed and shows the top recent quakes on its screen — with a full-screen alert if one crosses your chosen magnitude threshold. Follow these three steps to get it running.

STEP 01
Power On & Connect to Setup Wi-Fi
Plug the device into any standard USB power source. It shows a brief "SLOWTRONICS · Earthquake Tracker v4" splash, then checks for a saved network.
  • If no network is saved yet, the screen shows SETUP REQUIRED.
  • On your phone, tablet, or laptop, open Wi-Fi Settings.
  • Look for the network named:
    Setup-EQTracker
  • Connect to it. (No password required).

1. Connect Phone/PC to:

Setup-EQTracker

STEP 02
Configure Network & Alert Threshold
Once connected to Setup-EQTracker, a setup page should pop up automatically on your phone or browser.
Portal didn't open automatically?
Open your browser and go to:
http://192.168.4.1

Fill in the fields:

  1. WiFi Network: Pick your home network from the drop-down (shows live dBm).
  2. WiFi Password: Enter your home Wi-Fi password.
  3. Panic Threshold (Magnitude): Any quake at or above this magnitude within the last 10 minutes triggers the full-screen alert. Defaults to 4.5.
  4. Tap Save & Connect.

STEP 03
Save & Start Tracking!
After tapping "Save & Connect", the page confirms your settings were saved.

The device restarts, joins your home Wi-Fi, syncs the current time from the connection itself, and fetches the latest quakes from USGS. Within moments, the screen switches to the Live Dashboard.

Full-Screen Alert Mode
When a Quake Crosses Your Threshold
If any quake at or above your Panic Threshold happened in the last 10 minutes, the whole screen flips into a flashing red/magenta alert showing the magnitude, place, depth, and how long ago it struck. This:
  • Alternates between the alert screen and the normal dashboard every few seconds so you can still see the rest of the data.
  • Automatically clears itself after 5 minutes even if the quake is still "recent."
  • Always tells you to check official sources — it's a heads-up, not a substitute for official alerts.
Troubleshooting & Testing
Wrong Network or Moved Locations?
If the saved Wi-Fi network can't be reached after about 12 seconds of trying, the device automatically falls back into SETUP REQUIRED mode and starts broadcasting Setup-EQTracker again — just reconnect and repeat Step 2 with the new details.
Test the Alert Without Waiting for a Real Quake
With the device connected to your computer over USB:
  1. Open a Serial Monitor at 115200 baud.
  2. Send 1 to inject a simulated M6.5 quake and trigger the full alert screen.
  3. Send 0 to cancel the simulation and return to the normal dashboard.
